# Stokvel and Aid Terms of Agreement

This document serves as a guideline outlining the rules, participation details, and expectations for all members of the Stokvel. While this is not a legally binding contract, it ensures that all participants have a clear understanding of how the Stokvel operates and their responsibilities within it.

## Definitions.

- **Stokvel cycle**: The complete rotation of payouts, determined by the number of participants. Each cycle consists of every member receiving their total payout once. For example, in a Stokvel with six members and monthly contributions, a full cycle would last six months.
- **Contribution**: The fixed amount each member agrees to contribute at a set interval (e.g., monthly, bi-weekly) to the shared pool.
- **Payout**: The lump sum distributed to one member at the end of each contribution period until all members have received their payout for the cycle.
- **Aid Fund**: A reserved portion of contributions dedicated to assisting members in financial emergencies.
- **Overseer**: A designated person or group responsible for managing contributions, payouts, and resolving disputes.

## Purpose of the Stokvel

The Stokvel is designed to provide financial assistance and structured savings for all participating members. It serves two primary purposes:

1. **Rotational Payout System**: Each member contributes a fixed amount at a regular interval, and in return, they receive a lump sum payout during their designated period in the cycle. This system ensures that members can access a large sum of money for planned expenses, investments, or emergencies.
1. **Financial Aid Fund**: A supplementary system to help members in times of financial crisis through a structured aid program.

## Stokvel Structure

As explained the stokvel is a system that pays out the participants once per stokvel cycle. Each participant will have their chance to receive their stokvel payment.

### Payout Order

The payout order will be determined by considering each participant's financial status and trust hierarchy. This is to prevent the risk of a "receive and run" scenario in the first half of the cycle.

### Payout Date

The payout date will be determined by the last participant to contribute to the Stokvel that month. However, payments will only be withheld until the 10th of each month. If a participant misses this due date, penalties will be applied. (See Causes for Nil Compensation and Deposit).

### Stokvel Finance Holder

The finance holder should be the most trusted participant in the Stokvel. If a Double Stokvel is in effect, the same person cannot hold this role for both Stokvels. The finance holder has several responsibilities:

- Receiving and managing all contributions.
- Maintaining a separate account for aid funds.
- Processing payouts to members.
- Logging all transactions on the website with proof of payments.
- Ensuring transparency by providing proof of unhampered aid accounts.

## Financial Aid Option

This option is an additional voluntary contribution. Participants who opt in are eligible to request financial aid in emergencies. Aid is granted based on majority consent, allowing withdrawal of up to **75%** of the total aid fund and up to **50%** of the required amount for the emergency.

### What Qualifies as an Emergency?

Emergencies include but are not limited to:

- Medical expenses (doctor visits, medication, hospital stays, etc.).
- Unexpected financial instability (job loss, unexpected bills, etc.).
- Other pressing financial needs as determined by a vote.

### Financial Aid Limits

To ensure sustainability, the following limitations apply:

- If a participant withdraws more than 50% of the total aid fund, they will be temporarily barred from further aid requests until funds recover.
- They must continue making aid contributions during the barring period.
- Frequent use of the aid may result in an increased contribution requirement or temporary suspension from requesting aid.

### Financial Aid Claim Process

To claim financial aid, the following must be provided (if applicable):

- A written reason for the request.
- Bills or documentation proving the legitimacy of the request.
- Any supporting documents that strengthen the claim.

A meeting will be held to discuss the request, followed by an anonymous vote.

Before the meeting concludes, an anonymous [vote](#democratic-decision-making) will be held on wether the aid request should be accepted or not.

### Joining Financial Aid

A participant may join the financial aid fund at any time. Upon joining, they must pay a deposit determined by the overseers or defaulting to one month's Stokvel contribution. New members will only become eligible for aid requests after three months of contributions.

## Double Stokvel

Participants may be part of multiple Stokvels. However, the same individual cannot oversee multiple Stokvels unless approved by a vote.

## Website

A dedicated website will be developed to provide:

- A dashboard for participants to track their Stokvel memberships.
- Logs of Stokvel accounts and payouts (excluding individual contributions for privacy).
- An anonymous voting and results section.
- Administrative areas accessible only to overseers.

## Democratic Decision Making

Any participant can call for a vote on Stokvel-related matters. Revotes are limited to once per topic. Voting is anonymous to all except the overseer. Results will be visible to all members.

### Voting on Financial Aid

Only those who contribute to the financial aid fund may vote on aid-related decisions. This excludes overseers.

### Overseers

Overseers are responsible for managing the Stokvel and are often the finance holder. They may appoint a secondary overseer to assist them. Overseers have the authority to override votes in cases of suspected foul play. They can be replaced through a 75% majority vote.

## Stokvel Term Adjustments

The Stokvel terms are reviewed at the end of each cycle. Changes such as payment adjustments or payout frequency modifications can be proposed. A 75% majority vote is required for amendments during a cycle.

## Deposit

New members must pay a deposit equivalent to up to 50% of the participant count’s contribution. This serves as a fallback in case of early departure or missed payments.

## Trust and Accountability

A Stokvel relies on trust. Regular meetings will be held to maintain transparency and accountability. Participants who breach trust will be permanently removed and barred from future Stokvels.

## Regular Meetings

Monthly meetings will be held to strengthen trust, discuss concerns, and provide updates on the Stokvel’s progress. Attendance is encouraged but not mandatory.

## Early Exit Policy

Members who choose to leave mid-cycle forfeit their deposit. However, they may call for a vote to reclaim it, but only participants who have not yet received their payout may vote.

## Dispute Resolution

The overseers will assess disputes. If a decision cannot be reached by vote, the overseer's ruling will be final.
